# The Baker Who Survived the Titanic With Whiskey

**Title:** He Survived the Titanic Drunk 🥃🚢

## Script

**Hook (0-4s):** One man survived the Titanic's freezing water for hours. His secret? Whiskey.

**Story (4-35s):** April 15, 1912. Charles Joughin was the ship's chief baker.
As the Titanic sank, he threw deck chairs overboard so people had something to float on.
Then he went back below and drank. A lot.
When the ship went under, he rode the stern down like an elevator and stepped into the sea.
The water was minus two degrees. Most people died within fifteen minutes.

**Twist (35-48s):** Joughin paddled for over two hours. He said he barely felt the cold.
Some doctors think the alcohol helped. Others say he was simply lucky.

**Outro (48-56s):** He lived to the age of seventy-eight. Lucky man, or the luckiest drink in history? Comment below.

## Sound
- Music: slow, sad orchestral piano that turns tense as the ship sinks. Drop the music for a beat before the twist.
- SFX: ocean waves and wind under the story, a whoosh on "stepped into the sea", a ding on the final question.

## Visual Style
Vintage black and white photographs, 1912 newspaper look. A map pin on the North Atlantic. Sinking ship silhouette for the story.
